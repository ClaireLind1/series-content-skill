# Series English Editorial Workflow

This workflow applies across TV series and film. The Pitt articles, terminology, and builder in this
repository are one worked example; do not assume medical context or reuse show-specific facts for
other material.

## 1. Intake

Identify the work, episode or scene when available, intended reader, deliverable, and source status.
Notes may be exact lines, approximate transcriptions, expressions, words, or questions in any format.
Work on the user's selected items. Do not claim to have watched a video or obtained unprovided subtitles.

## 2. Organize selected material

Explain scene meaning, tone, and language use item by item. Add terminology, abbreviations,
pronunciation, collocations, Chinese explanations, and teaching examples only when useful. Distinguish
show dialogue, user transcription, paraphrase, and newly written examples. Mark unverified wording for
verification. Check specialist claims against reliable sources; a medical drama is not a clinical guide.

## 3. Plan or draft

Match scope to the request. For planning, deliver an angle, context notes, outline, and verification
items. For a requested article, organize reviewed items for the intended reader. A useful sequence may
be `scene context → expressions → usage/examples → recap`, but adjust it to the material rather than
forcing fixed sections or length.

## 4. Generate WeChat output

`pitt_t07_layout.py` and `build-the-pitt-*.py` are example implementations for The Pitt medical-English
series. Reuse them only for suitable Pitt work. For other shows, verify the schema and layout before
adapting; do not treat medical terms or show-specific fields as universal. Include a “Copy formatted
content” button in WeChat HTML and keep it consistent with the source article.

## 5. Review and deliver

Check source status, scene fit, terms and translations, quotation/example distinctions, structure,
HTML output, and links. State changed files, regeneration steps, and unresolved checks. Do not claim
paste or dark-mode validation without testing it in the Official Account editor.

## WeChat Official Account

For HTML deliverables, tell the user to click “Copy formatted content,” sign in at
[mp.weixin.qq.com](https://mp.weixin.qq.com/), create a new article, and paste the body. The title,
author, summary, and cover are filled in separately. Preview, save a draft, and publish when ready.
Copying the body does not populate those fields or upload a cover.
