# Project guidance

- This Skill turns user-provided notes and drafts into articles and WeChat layouts. Support reading, courses, work and knowledge sharing; The Pitt is one case study.
- Match scope: layout-only preserves text; outline-only and review-only requests do not authorize full rewrites.
- Preserve the user's voice and claims; do not invent experiences, facts, quotes or unseen source content.
- Verify specialist claims as needed and distinguish quotations, paraphrases and authored examples.
- Use bundled templates for HTML with a rich-text copy action outside the article. Adapt topic labels and branding; do not force medical learning structures onto general content.
- The Pitt uses the approved minimal Clinical Editorial template. Other topics follow the user's chosen style.
- Quizzes, advertising, resource downloads and conversion modules are optional. Do not claim publication or WeChat paste validation without evidence.
