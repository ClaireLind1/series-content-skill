# Project guidance

- The reusable workflow is for English notes from a TV series or film. The Pitt files in `examples/` are one medical-drama case study, not assumptions for other titles.
- Work from dialogue, expressions, notes, or questions the user selected. Do not imply that the Skill watches episodes or discovers unprovided material.
- Keep exact quotations, approximate transcription, paraphrase, and original teaching examples distinct. Mark uncertain wording or context for review.
- Explain language in context. Verify specialized factual claims when needed; treat medical details as learning context, not clinical guidance.
- Match the requested scope: notes, outline, draft, article HTML, or review. Do not expand a request for an outline or review into a full article.
- For WeChat HTML, preserve approved article content and provide the “Copy formatted content” action when supported by the renderer.
